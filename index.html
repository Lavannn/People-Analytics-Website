How I Think About People Analytics

Analytics that shifts from data production to insight generation

Reports describe what happened. Decision-ready analytics tells leadership what to do about it - with the method, the assumptions and the confidence level shown, not hidden behind a dashboard.

That means pairing statistical rigor (regression, survival analysis, experimental design) with a clear line of sight to the business decision it's meant to inform, and communicating it in language an Executive Committee can act on in the room.
