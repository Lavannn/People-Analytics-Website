About

Nineteen years learning how organizations actually make workforce decisions

A People Analytics & HR Transformation strategist with 19+ years of progressive experience delivering actionable people insights that enhance employee experience and drive organizational performance - across Big 4 consulting, engineering, IT, retail, manufacturing, real estate and aviation.

The throughline across every role: shifting analytics delivery from data production to insight generation, so teams produce clear, decision-ready recommendations rather than disconnected reports — and presenting those recommendations directly to Executive Committees and senior leadership.
